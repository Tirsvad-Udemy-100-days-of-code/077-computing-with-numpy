# 077 Computing with NumPy

Day 77 of Udemy's "100 Days of Code: The Complete Python Pro Bootcamp" teaches NumPy, the standard library for numerical data in Python. This wiki walks through every lecture step and explains what you gain from it. Each step has a screenshot of the real output of the repository's Jupyter notebook.

## What you gain

- A way of thinking in arrays: whole-array operations instead of loops.
- Confidence with indexing and slicing in any number of dimensions.
- The linear algebra the data science course builds on.
- A tested repository you can run, read and compare with your own solution.
