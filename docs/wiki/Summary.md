## Learning points

- Create arrays with `np.array`, `.arange`, `.random` and `.linspace`.
- Read `shape` and `ndim`, and slice by indices.
- Use broadcasting to make shapes compatible.
- Do linear algebra with scalars and matrix multiplication.
- Manipulate images as ndarrays.
